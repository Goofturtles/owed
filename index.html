Questions people ask before they claim.

Short answers. The full rule is one tap away in the app.

Is this actually free?

Yes. Owed takes no cut of anything you claim and sells no warranties. It is a student project built to be free.

Do I need my receipt?

Not to check. Roughly when you bought it is enough. To claim you will usually need proof, and a bank statement line often counts, which is why Owed asks how you paid.

What if it finds nothing for my thing?

It says so plainly. The rules are collected by hand, so an unusual brand may come back empty. Anything on your shelf is re-checked every time you open Owed.

Is this legal advice?

No. Owed points at published rules and tells you what to ask, and every result links to its source. Strong, worth asking and long shot rate how well a rule fits your answers, not whether you qualify.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Your shelf and your sign-in live in your own browser’s storage. There is no server collecting what you own.

Which countries does it work in?

The US, Canada, the UK and the EU. Maker warranties lean American and Canadian, and card benefits are almost all North American. Pick your country and Owed filters to it; state rules are named by state.
